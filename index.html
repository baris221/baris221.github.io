Research

Knapsack Problem

The binary ordinal knapsack problem involves items whose values are assessed on a qualitative scale rather than a numerical scale.

BUCO Problem

The biobjective unconstrained combinatorial optimization problem (BUCO) can be viewed as a variant of the knapsack problem where the capacity constraint is converted into an objective function.

Ordinal Assignment

Assignment problems in which agents express preferences over tasks through ordinal rankings rather than cardinal utilities.
